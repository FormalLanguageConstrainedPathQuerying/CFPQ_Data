### S2: Document the RPQ design decision in flpq.rst

**Code:** none.
**Tests:** docs build exits 0.
**Docs:** `docs/flpq.rst` — a new "Regular path queries" section right after
"Query classes" (taxonomy order), plus the taxonomy table: the RPQ row's
"Status in the dataset" changes from "planned" to "designed, no data".

**Spec:**
- The section records the design decision, in this order:
  - **Format.** RPQ queries are regular expressions over the graph's edge
    labels in the pyformlang `Regex` text syntax (``.re`` files), read by the
    existing reader (`cfpq_data/grammars/readwrite/regex.py`) — referenced,
    not re-specified.
  - **Templates.** As with CFG templates, an RPQ template is a named,
    parameterized regular expression instantiated per graph from its stored
    labels. The designed seed set (canonical queries applicable to every
    graph):
    1. `reachability` — `(L_1 | ... | L_n)^*` over all stored labels
       (unrestricted reachability);
    2. `label_star` — `L^*` for each stored label `L` (per-label
       transitive closure).
    Additional templates may be added later together with real-world data.
  - **Placement.** Query files live inside the self-contained graph archive
    under `queries/rpq/` (referenced from the "File structure" section of
    the graphs page); there are no standalone query or example archives.
  - **Data status.** No real-world RPQ data exists yet — the templates are
    designed stubs; real-world data will be provided later and enters the
    dataset via the graph archives (migration, task 48).
- Nothing in this section duplicates the format paragraph, the taxonomy
  table, or the "Dataset layout and migration" section — it references them.
